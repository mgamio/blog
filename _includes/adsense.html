{%- comment -%} Deferred loader for Google's scripts (BLOG_AUTOMATION.md §4.2). IDs come from _config.yml. gtag.js (GA4 + Google Ads, production only) and adsbygoogle.js (AdSense and Google's consent banner) load on the first interaction or 3 s after load, whichever comes first, to keep them off the critical path (FCP/LCP/TBT).
