Sec. 06 — About the Author

Written from the field, not the whiteboard.

Moises Gamio has spent 25 years as a software engineer across government, financial, industrial, education, consulting, retail and research organizations — working in Java, C, COBOL, C#, Visual Basic, PHP, Python and JScript, across Windows, Linux, Unix and RISC/6000, against SQL Server, Oracle, MySQL, DB2 and Lotus Notes.

Eight of those years were spent as a systems analyst in the banking sector. Since 2014, he has been building web services for a B2B company in Berlin, Germany — the same real-world pressure that shaped every principle in this book.
