The easiest way to reach me is by email:

<p class="contact-email"><a href="mailto:codersitedev@gmail.com">codersitedev@gmail.com</a></p>

I read every message and usually answer within a few days.

## What you can write to me about

- **Questions about an article or a tool**, or ideas for a new one.
- **Corrections.** If you find a mistake in a post or in a code example, please tell me which page and what is wrong; I fix them quickly.
- **My books.** Questions about [The Code Interview](/book/theCodeInterview.html) or [Software That Can Change](/book/softwareDesignPrinciplesV2.html). If you bought a PDF and didn't receive the download email, include the email address you used at checkout.
- **Collaborations**, such as guest articles, partnerships or consulting.
- **Privacy requests**, for example to access or delete your data. See the [privacy policy](/policy) for your rights.

Please don't send passwords, API keys or other secrets in your message.

The legal details about this website are in the [Impressum](/impressum).
