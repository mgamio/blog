This policy explains which personal data is processed when you visit codersite.dev, why, and what rights you have. I keep it short and specific: if something isn't listed here, this site doesn't do it.

## Hosting and server logs {#hosting}

This website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service of GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser type. GitHub needs this data to deliver the website and to keep it secure, and may store it in server logs.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a secure, working website). GitHub may process data in the USA; it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

All scripts, styles and fonts of the blog itself are served from codersite.dev. The exceptions are the third-party services described below.

## Amazon affiliate links {#amazon}

**As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.** Many articles link to books and products on Amazon. These are affiliate links of the Amazon Associates program: if you buy something after clicking one, I receive a small commission. The price is the same for you.

Nothing is loaded from Amazon until you click a link. When you click, you leave codersite.dev, and Amazon may set a cookie to record that you came from this site. I receive only aggregated reports about clicks and sales, never your name or what else you bought. Amazon is responsible for the data it processes: [Amazon Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.de/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

Legal basis for showing affiliate links: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in financing this website). The footer of every page repeats this notice.

## E-book sales (Payhip) {#payhip}

My e-books are sold through **Payhip** (Payhip Ltd, United Kingdom). On the book pages, a Payhip script is loaded to show the checkout window; this transmits your IP address and browser data to Payhip. If you buy, Payhip and its payment providers (for example Stripe or PayPal) process your name, email address and payment data to complete the order and send you the download link. I receive your name, email address and order details as the seller.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performing the purchase contract). More information: [Payhip Privacy Policy](https://payhip.com/privacy){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

## Online shop (Spreadshop) {#shop}

Our shop page ([codersite.dev/shop](/shop)) embeds our Spreadshop (codersite.myspreadshop.de), which is operated by sprd.net AG ("Spreadshirt"), Gießerstraße 27, 04229 Leipzig, Germany. When you open the shop page, your browser loads scripts and content from Spreadshirt's servers, and Spreadshirt receives technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page you came from and the time of the request. Other pages of codersite.dev do not load the shop.

Spreadshirt shows its own cookie banner on the shop page. Cookies that are necessary to run the shop and the basket are set on the basis of our legitimate interest in offering our merchandise (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Other cookies and technologies, such as analytics and marketing, are only used if you agree in Spreadshirt's banner (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). You can refuse them and still use the shop, and you can change your choice at any time in Spreadshirt's cookie settings.

If you place an order, your contract is exclusively with sprd.net AG, not with codersite.dev. Spreadshirt processes your order, payment, shipping and returns, and the personal data required for them, under its own responsibility. More information: [Spreadshirt privacy policy](https://service.spreadshirt.com/hc/de/articles/115000978409){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

## Your rights {#rights}

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data I hold about you (Art. 15),
- have incorrect data **corrected** (Art. 16),
- have your data **deleted** (Art. 17) or its processing **restricted** (Art. 18),
- receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20),
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21),
- **withdraw your consent** at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)), using the "Change privacy settings" button above.

To exercise these rights, email [codersitedev@gmail.com](mailto:codersitedev@gmail.com). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

**California residents:** I don't sell personal information. You can ask me which personal information I hold about you and ask me to delete it by emailing the address above.
