I have worked as a software developer in Berlin for more than ten years. This list contains the German words I hear and use most often in meetings, documents, emails, tickets and code reviews, each with its article and English translation. I update it from time to time.

**Three tips before you start:**

- **Learn every noun with its article.** Nouns ending in *-ung, -heit, -keit, -tät* and *-ion* are always *die*: die Anwendung, die Sicherheit, die Qualität, die Version.
- **Long words are just short words put together.** *Die Qualitätssicherung* is *die Qualität* (quality) plus *die Sicherung* (assurance). The last word decides the article.
- **Many teams speak "Denglisch".** In Berlin especially, people say *deployen, committen, mergen, das Deployment, der Sprint, das Backlog* without translating them. You don't need a German word for everything.
